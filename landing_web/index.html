A LITTLE HELP GETTING STARTED.

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Here are a few things to know before you join.

How do I install the Android app?+

Tap Download the app, then open app-release.apk from your phone’s Downloads. If Android asks, allow this browser or file manager to install this app, then tap Install. You can turn that permission off again afterward.

Who is E-SUGID for?+

E-SUGID is made for residents of Barili, Cebu, across its 42 barangays. Local officials and organization staff use the separate admin console to manage their services.

Is the app free? Is there an iPhone version?+

The Android app is free to download. An internet connection is needed for online features, and your mobile provider’s data charges may apply. This download is for Android; an iPhone version is not available here.

What do I need to create an account?+

You must be at least 15 years old. Enter your details and barangay, then verify your email using the code sent to your inbox. Some services also require resident verification.

Where do barangay and organization staff log in?+

Use Admin Login at the top of this page to open the web console. You’ll need an authorized staff account.
