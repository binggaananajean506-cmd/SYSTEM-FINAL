# Distinct CFA grounds

Reference: [DILG KP forms, pages 22–23](https://region5.dilg.gov.ph/albay/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/KP-Forms.pdf). The official forms distinguish failed mediation/conciliation (20-A) from unjustified respondent nonappearance before the Pangkat (20-B). Both use Pangkat secretary and chairperson signers. The application templates paraphrase the respective findings; issuing officials must verify the legal grounds and supporting office records.

Preserve the user's six-round local workflow and existing manual issue step. Choose 20-A or 20-B explicitly. Do not infer unjustified absence, proper service, or constitution of the Pangkat from a tanod handoff or an absence field. 20-A requires a failed personal confrontation in the barangay phase and a completed final Pangkat round with both parties present and no agreement. 20-B requires the final Pangkat round to record the complainant present and respondent absent, plus an explicit official confirmation of proper notice, Pangkat proceedings, and willful absence/refusal without a valid reason. Neither is available when the final round records the complainant absent or attendance unknown. Earlier absence is not reclassified as agreement or attendance.

Legacy counts with incomplete attendance require an additional explicit check of paper records; they do not automatically prove either ground. The server validates the selected form and stores the form, ground, review confirmations, signer names, issuance date and actor. Names are optional and blank signature lines remain available for manual completion; no captain or secretary is assumed to hold a Pangkat role. Preview, print and PDF share the same stored form model. Already-issued records without a stored variant retain their previous Form 20 label and recorded grounds; never retrospectively assign a new legal form based on today's attendance.

The existing CFA status, privacy, authorization, audit and idempotent operation checks remain enforced.
